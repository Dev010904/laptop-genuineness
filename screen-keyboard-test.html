Screen and Keyboard Test

Screen test: goes full screen. Click or press Space / arrow keys to change colour. Esc to exit.

  • White, red, green, blue: look for black or coloured dots (dead or stuck pixels).
  • Black: dim the room lights, look for glowing edges or corners (backlight bleed).
  • Grey: look for faint ghost shapes (image retention from the store demo loop) and patchy areas.
  • Gradient: should be smooth with no visible stripes.

Measuring refresh rate…

Press every key. Each one turns green when it works.
